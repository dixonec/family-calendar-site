Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. This policy describes the personal Family Calendar desktop app.

Data accessed and used

When you connect Google, the app accesses your account email, calendar list, and event data to display and synchronize calendars and save edits you request. New sign-ins request permission to read the list of calendars you subscribe to and to view and edit events on calendars you can access. The app does not request permission to manage calendar sharing or delete whole calendars. Earlier versions requested full Calendar access; existing grants may retain that broader permission until you revoke them in your Google Account. iCloud connections use your Apple ID and an app-specific password to access your calendars through CalDAV.

Local storage and transmission

Calendar data, account identifiers, authorization tokens, the Google desktop client configuration, and iCloud app-specific passwords are stored in the app's local application-support file on your device. This file is not separately encrypted by the app or stored in Keychain. Your operating system and any device backups govern access to this local data.

The app communicates directly with Google and Apple to authenticate and synchronize. It has no developer-operated calendar server, advertising, or analytics service. Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. If you choose to share an event, the selected sharing application receives the content you share. Local reminder notifications may display event information on your device.

Your controls

Removing a connected account in Accounts removes its saved account credentials and imported events from the app. Calendar labels and other local settings may remain. Removing an account locally does not delete its events at the provider or revoke provider access. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections and revoke an iCloud app-specific password in your Apple Account settings. The app's local data file and any backups can be removed separately to erase remaining local data.

Limited use and contact

Family Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions or removal help, contact the app owner using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process ordinary web request data under its own privacy statement.
